How Do I Help a Friend?

Learn how to support someone you care about through their mental health journey.

Understanding Mental Health

Mental health affects how we think, feel, and act. Many people struggle silently, so being informed helps you support someone with empathy and compassion.

  • 1 in 5 people experience mental health challenges each year.
  • You don’t have to be an expert to help—just being present matters.
  • Listening without judgment can make a huge difference.

How to Support a Friend

  • Check in regularly, even with a simple text like “Thinking of you.”
  • Encourage them to talk, but don’t push if they aren’t ready.
  • Help them with small tasks when life feels overwhelming.
  • Celebrate their progress, no matter how small.
  • Remind them they’re not a burden.

When to Encourage Professional Help

If your friend is struggling to cope or showing signs of depression, anxiety, or self-harm, gently suggest talking to a counselor or mental health professional.

  • Offer to help them research therapists.
  • Walk with them to the school counselor or health center.
  • Reassure them that seeking help is a sign of strength.
