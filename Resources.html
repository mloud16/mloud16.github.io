Support & Resources

You are never alone. Help is always available.

📞 Immediate Help

If you or someone you know is in crisis, reach out right now. Someone is ready to listen.

988 — Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (U.S.)

Available 24/7 • Free • Confidential

💗 Additional Support Resources

  • Crisis Text Line — Text **HOME** to **741741**
  • The Trevor Project — 24/7 support for LGBTQ+ youth
  • NAMI — National Alliance on Mental Illness resources & education
  • SAMHSA National Helpline — 1-800-662-HELP (4357)
  • MentalHealth.gov — Guidance, info, and support

🌱 Finding Community

Struggling can feel isolating, but you’re part of a much larger story. Many people are fighting similar battles, and connecting with others can provide strength.

You can also create a safe space on this site where visitors can share stories, express emotions, or simply breathe and take in supportive words.
