Mental Health is Health

Mental health is a state of emotional, psychological, and social well-being that affects how we think, feel, and act, and it encompasses three main components: cognitive, emotional, and behavioral health. It is important at every stage of life, from childhood to adulthood, and includes the ability to cope with stress, learn, work, and contribute to one's community. Our emotional health can range from thriving to struggling. No matter what you're experiencing, there are ways to take action to support yourself and those around you.

Addressing Mental Health

Addressing mental health is crucial for overall well-being. It involves recognizing the importance of mental health, reducing stigma, and promoting access to care. Strategies include education, early intervention, and support systems. By prioritizing mental health, individuals can lead healthier, more fulfilling lives.

Impact

Did you know?

  • 1 in 5 adults in the U.S. experience mental illness each year.
  • 1 in 6 youth aged 6-17 experience a mental health disorder each year.
  • 50% of all lifetime mental illness begins by age 14.
  • Only 43% of adults with mental illness receive treatment.
  • Mental health conditions can affect relationships, school, and work.
