Feeling Anxious

Anxiety can feel overwhelming, but you're not alone. It's your body's response to stress or uncertainty.

Tips:

  • Practice slow, deep breathing.
  • Try grounding techniques (5-4-3-2-1 method).
  • Talk to someone you trust.

Resources:

  • Calm App
  • Anxiety & Depression Association of America (ADAA)

Feeling Sad

Sadness is a normal human emotion. It can be triggered by many things, and it's okay to feel this way.

Tips:

  • Write down your thoughts.
  • Put on calming or uplifting music.
  • Reach out to a friend.

Resources:

  • BetterHelp
  • Mental Health America

Feeling Overwhelmed

When life feels like too much, your brain may struggle to process everything at once.

Tips:

  • Break tasks into small steps.
  • Take a short walk or get fresh air.
  • Give yourself permission to pause.

Resources:

  • Headspace

Feeling Angry

Anger is a natural emotion that can signal when something is wrong or unfair.

Tips:

  • Count to ten before reacting.
  • Engage in physical activity.
  • Practice relaxation techniques.

Resources:

  • Anger Management Techniques - Mayo Clinic
